NVR Disk Calculator

Size the video Data Disk and the OS/DB System Disk separately

Data Disk (Video)

Holds the recorded video files from camera RTSP streams. Grows to many TB with channels, bitrate and retention. Usually large HDDs.

System Disk (OS · DB)

Holds the OS/Docker images and PostgreSQL metadata (segment index, events). It is an index, not video, so only tens of GB. Usually an SSD.

Formula
[Data Disk · Video]
Daily(GB) = Bitrate(Mbps) × (FPS ÷ 30) × 3,600 × Hours(h) × Channels ÷ 8 ÷ 1,000
Data Disk(TB) = Daily(GB) × Days ÷ 1,000

[System Disk · PostgreSQL metadata]
Segment rows = 60 × Hours(h) × Days × Channels  (1-minute segments, per track)
Metadata(GB) = rows × rowSize(enc 5KB / plain 3KB) ÷ 10⁹ × (1 + activity%) × 1.25
          (+1.5KB/row per track if audio · 1.25 = WAL/index/VACUUM headroom)
System Disk(GB) = (Base + Metadata) × (1 + margin%)

※ Bitrate presets are based on 30fps; changing FPS scales proportionally.
※ Video is never stored in PostgreSQL (metadata only).
